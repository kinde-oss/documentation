<Aside type="upgrade">

Organization-managed roles and permissions are available on the [Kinde Scale plan](https://kinde.com/pricing/).

</Aside>

By default, [roles](/manage-users/roles-and-permissions/user-roles/) and [permissions](/manage-users/roles-and-permissions/user-permissions/) are defined once for your whole business and shared by every organization. On the Scale plan, you can also let each organization define roles and permissions of its own. This is useful when your customers need role structures you cannot predict, for example a customer who wants a `billing-approver` role that only exists in their organization.

This topic explains how organization-managed roles work, how to control which permissions organizations can use, and how to manage organization roles in Kinde, in the self-serve portal, and through the Kinde Management API.

## How organization-managed roles work

- **Business-wide roles still apply.** Roles you define under **Settings > User management > Roles** are available in every organization. Organization roles are added on top of them.
- **Organization roles belong to one organization.** An organization can assign a role it creates only to its own members. Other organizations never see it.
- **Organization roles hold two kinds of permissions.** The organization's own permissions, and the business-wide permissions you have marked as **Available to organization roles**. An organization cannot add any other business-wide permission to its roles.
- **Organization permissions only reach users through organization roles.** You cannot grant an organization permission directly to a user. Add it to one of the organization's roles and assign the role instead.
- **They work in tokens like any other role or permission.** Their permissions appear in the `permissions` claim alongside business-wide permissions, and the roles themselves appear in the `roles` claim once you [add roles to the access token](/manage-users/roles-and-permissions/user-roles/#add-roles-to-the-access-token) for the application. Your application code does not need to distinguish them.
- **Keys must be unique.** An organization role or permission key must be unique within that organization, and it cannot match a business-wide key. Likewise, you cannot create a business-wide role or permission with a key that an organization is already using. Keys cannot be changed after creation.
- **Limits apply.** Each organization can have up to 100 roles and 100 permissions of its own. Names and keys can be up to 64 characters, and descriptions up to 255 characters.
- **Organization roles do not affect MFA exemptions.** Only business-wide roles can be used as [MFA exempt roles](/authenticate/multi-factor-auth/mfa-per-org/) for an organization.

## Choose which business permissions organizations can use

Before an organization can build roles from your business-wide permissions, you need to mark those permissions as available to organization roles. Permissions that are not marked stay reserved for business-wide roles.

1. In Kinde, go to **Settings > User management > Permissions**.
2. Select **Add permission**, or select the three dots next to an existing permission and choose **Edit permission**.

    ![add permission setting screen](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/c8da2ccd-734c-446e-5778-62c24b0dc000/socialsharingimage)

3. Switch on **Available to organization roles**.

    ![add permission dialogue](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/5ec03a68-7c6b-431a-278b-7d8d4e0f4700/socialsharingimage)

4. Select **Save**.

<Aside type="warning">

If you switch **Available to organization roles** off for a permission, it is removed from every organization role that includes it, in every environment. Members who have those roles lose the permission the next time their token is refreshed, unless a business-wide role they hold, or a direct grant, still grants it.

</Aside>

## Add an organization permission in Kinde

An organization permission can only be used in that organization's roles.

1. In Kinde, go to **Organizations** and select the organization you want to modify.
2. In the side menu, select **Roles**.

    ![org add role or permission screen empty](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/599806ba-42d5-4985-58c1-27fe358e8400/socialsharingimage)

3. Select **Add permission**.
4. Give the permission a **Name**, for example 'Approve invoices'.
5. Enter a **Key**, for example `approve:invoices`. The key must be unique within the organization and cannot be changed later.
6. Enter a **Description**.

    ![organization add permissions screen](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/680c60fb-0aec-424f-e649-44c05353a800/socialsharingimage)

7. Select **Save**. The permission can now be added to the organization's roles.

## Add an organization role in Kinde

1. In Kinde, go to **Organizations** and select the organization.
2. In the side menu, select **Roles**. The page lists the roles and permissions that belong only to this organization.

    ![organization add role setting screen](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/9a9c876a-5d53-47fb-ed76-6bf728b2a300/socialsharingimage)

3. Select **Add role**.
4. Give the role a **Name**. Keep it short and descriptive, for example 'Billing approver'.
5. Enter a **Key**. This is how the role appears in tokens, for example `billing-approver`. The key must be unique within the organization and cannot be changed later.
6. Enter a **Description** of what the role is for and who should have it.
7. Switch on the **Permissions** to include in the role. The list shows the business-wide permissions you have made available to organization roles, and this organization's own permissions.

    ![organization edit role setting screen](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/f0a0e6cf-dcfb-4703-25c5-d99253ffc300/socialsharingimage)

8. Select **Save**.

<Aside>

If no permissions are listed, the organization has no permissions of its own yet, and no business-wide permissions have been made available to organization roles. Add an organization permission, or mark a business-wide permission as available, before you create the role.

</Aside>

## Edit or delete organization roles and permissions

Like business-wide roles, changes apply to every member who has the role the next time their token is refreshed.

1. In Kinde, go to **Organizations** and select the organization.
2. In the side menu, select **Roles**.
3. Select the three dots next to the role or permission and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

    ![organization edit or delete role setting screen](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/ed0d9803-d239-4a70-fd5a-583af2a83300/socialsharingimage)

4. Make your changes and select **Save**, or confirm the deletion.

    <Aside type="danger" title="Deleting roles and permissions is permanent">

    Deleting an organization role removes it from every member who has it, and from the organization's default roles. Deleting an organization permission removes it from every organization role that includes it. Neither deletion can be reversed.

    </Aside>

## Assign organization roles to members

Organization roles are assigned the same way as business-wide roles. See [Apply roles and permissions to users](/manage-users/roles-and-permissions/apply-roles-and-permissions-to-users/).

- In Kinde, open the user, select **Roles**, choose the organization, and switch on the role. The organization's own roles are listed alongside your business-wide roles for that organization.
- In the self-serve portal, authorized members can assign organization roles when they [invite a member](/build/self-service-portal/self-serve-portal-for-orgs/#invite-members-from-the-portal) or edit a member's roles.
- On the Scale plan with advanced organization features, you can also make an organization role a [default role for the organization](/manage-users/roles-and-permissions/default-user-roles/#enable-default-roles-in-an-organization), so new members receive it automatically.

## Let organization admins manage roles in the self-serve portal

You can let an organization's admins create and manage roles and permissions for that organization without contacting you.

1. In Kinde, go to **Settings > Environment > Self-serve portal**. To enable this for a single organization instead, see [Customize the self-serve portal for an organization](/build/organizations/self-serve-portal-per-org/).
2. In the **Organization self-management** section, switch on **Roles**. The switch is off by default.

    ![roles in org self-serve portal settings](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/a339bb3a-9f44-4b1c-0572-ead1e2557e00/socialsharingimage)

3. Select **Save**.

Access inside the portal is controlled by two system permissions:

- `org:read:roles` lets a member view the organization's roles and permissions.
- `org:write:roles` lets a member create, edit, and delete them.

The Kinde-managed `owner` role includes both. To give other members access, add these permissions to the roles you assign to them. See [Portal access control with system permissions](/build/self-service-portal/self-serve-portal-for-orgs/#portal-access-control-with-system-permissions).

In the portal, members with access open **Roles** to add roles and permissions, and open **Members** to assign roles.

## Manage organization roles via the Kinde Management API

Organization roles and permissions have their own endpoints under the organization. Business-wide endpoints such as [List roles](/kinde-apis/management#tag/roles/get/api/v1/roles) and [List permissions](/kinde-apis/management#tag/permissions/get/api/v1/permissions) return business-wide roles and permissions only.

| Action | Endpoint | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List organization roles | `GET /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/roles` | `read:organization_roles` |
| Add organization role | `POST /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/roles` | `create:organization_roles` |
| Get organization role | `GET /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/roles/{role_id}` | `read:organization_roles` |
| Update organization role | `PATCH /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/roles/{role_id}` | `update:organization_roles` |
| Delete organization role | `DELETE /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/roles/{role_id}` | `delete:organization_roles` |
| List organization permissions | `GET /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/permissions` | `read:organization_roles` |
| Add organization permission | `POST /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/permissions` | `create:organization_roles` |
| Get organization permission | `GET /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/permissions/{permission_id}` | `read:organization_roles` |
| Update organization permission | `PATCH /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/permissions/{permission_id}` | `update:organization_roles` |
| Delete organization permission | `DELETE /api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/permissions/{permission_id}` | `delete:organization_roles` |

See the [Organizations](/kinde-apis/management#tag/organizations) section of the Management API reference for request and response details.

Things to know when using the API:

- To make a business-wide permission available to organization roles, set `is_organization_role_assignable` to `true` when you [add](/kinde-apis/management#tag/permissions/post/api/v1/permissions) or [update](/kinde-apis/management#tag/permissions/patch/api/v1/permissions/{permission_id}) the permission. The field is also returned when you list permissions.
- When you add a role, `permissions` is an array of permission IDs. The request fails with `PERMISSION_NOT_ASSIGNABLE` if any of them is not available to the organization's roles. When you update a role, `permissions` replaces the role's entire permission set.
- Role and permission keys cannot be changed after creation. A `PATCH` that sends a different `key` fails with `KEY_IMMUTABLE`.
- When you [add users to an organization](/kinde-apis/management#tag/organizations/post/api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/users) or [update their roles](/kinde-apis/management#tag/organizations/patch/api/v1/organizations/{org_code}/users), a role key can be a business-wide role or one of that organization's own roles. A key that is neither fails with `INVALID_ROLES`.
- Adding a role or permission for an organization fails with `PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED` if your business is not on the Scale plan. Listing and deleting still work, so you can clean up after a downgrade.

## Webhooks

Organization roles and permissions use the same `role.created`, `role.updated`, `role.deleted`, `permission.created`, `permission.updated`, and `permission.deleted` [webhook events](/integrate/webhooks/add-manage-webhooks/) as business-wide roles and permissions. When the role or permission belongs to an organization, `data.role` or `data.permission` includes an `organization_code` field. Business-wide permission events include `is_organization_role_assignable` instead.

Here is a decoded `role.created` event for an organization role. See [About webhooks](/integrate/webhooks/about-webhooks/) for the full payload format.

```json
{
  "data": {
    "role": {
      "id": "018f3d4e-6f2a-7a1b-9c3d-2e4f5a6b7c8d",
      "name": "Billing approver",
      "key": "billing-approver",
      "description": "Can approve invoices for this organization",
      "organization_code": "org_7d8e9f0a1b2c"
    }
  },
  "event_id": "event_1234567890",
  "event_timestamp": "2026-10-27T12:00:00.000Z",
  "source": "admin",
  "timestamp": "2026-10-27T12:00:00.000Z",
  "type": "role.created"
}
```

## What happens if you move off the Scale plan

If your business moves to a plan without organization-managed roles:

- Existing organization roles and permissions are kept, but they are no longer included in tokens or permission checks. Members keep their assignments in Kinde, and those roles and permissions are included again if you upgrade.
- You cannot add or edit organization roles and permissions in Kinde, in the portal, or through the API. You can still delete them.
- The **Roles** page is hidden in the self-serve portal.
- In Kinde, the organization's **Roles** tab shows an upgrade banner.
