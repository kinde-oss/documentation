If you offer different levels of paid plans, then you will need to facilitate plan upgrade or downgrade. Before you make plan changes, make sure you have set the policies. 

## Set billing policies for cancellation and plan changes

You can also set policies to control what happens when a customer cancels or changes their plan. The policies are for deciding if you charge or forgive unpaid usage of metered features, and if you refund or retain unused, paid subscription days. 

By default, unpaid usage is forgiven and you don't refund or credit for unused subscription days.

1. Go to **Settings > Environment > Billing** to set these policy defaults.
2. In the **Policies** section, choose the settings.
   ![Billing policies settings](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/1a4c3673-d972-4dd6-2c3a-d46b9eb70200/public)
3. Select **Save**.

<Aside>

These policies apply to recurring subscription charges and metered features. Active [one-time purchases](/billing/manage-subscribers/one-time-purchases/#plan-changes-and-cancellations) belong to the current billing agreement and are always forfeited without a refund when a customer changes plan or cancels, regardless of these policy settings.

</Aside>

### Immediate and scheduled cancellations

These policies apply when a subscription is cancelled **immediately**.

If the plan's **Cancellation takes effect** setting (or the timing you choose in admin or the API) is the end of a billing period:

- The customer keeps access until that date.
- Stripe always issues the final period invoice at the effective date. The invoice-now policy does not apply to a scheduled cancel.
- Unpaid usage up to that date can still be charged, depending on your usage policy.
- Unused prepaid days are not refunded at request time — the customer is using what they already paid for.
- [One-time purchases](/billing/manage-subscribers/one-time-purchases/#plan-changes-and-cancellations) stay until the agreement actually ends.

See [Cancel a subscription](/billing/manage-plans/cancel-plans/#when-a-cancellation-takes-effect).
