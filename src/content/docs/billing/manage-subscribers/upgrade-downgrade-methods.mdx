Customers will eventually want to change the plan they are on and you need a way to alter their subscription.

## How to upgrade or downgrade a plan subscription

- Set up the self-serve portal and allow customers to manage their own subscriptions. See [self-serve portal for orgs](/build/self-service-portal/self-serve-portal-for-orgs/) and [self-serve portal for users](/build/self-service-portal/self-serve-portal-for-users/). In this scenario, the user selects the new plan from the pricing table. If any displayed plan offers annual pricing, they can turn on **Pay annually** to see and select yearly (or mixed yearly + monthly) prices — the same toggle used at sign-up. See [How customers choose annual pricing](/billing/billing-user-experience/plan-selection/#how-customers-choose-annual-pricing). Self-serve changes stay within the pricing table’s plan group.
- Manually upgrade or downgrade via the Kinde UI (see below). The **Change plan** dialog lists published plans by plan group, so you can move a customer to a plan in a different group of the same type (organization or user). Choosing annual pricing when an admin assigns or changes a plan is not available in this flow yet.
- Overwrite the current agreement (subscription) by [creating a new one via the Management API](/kinde-apis/management/#tag/billing-agreements/post/api/v1/billing/agreements/). The new plan can be in a different plan group. The current agreement is cancelled even if it belongs to a different group. The new plan must be the same customer type as the customer (organization or user).

<Aside>

Self-serve **Pay annually** applies when moving to a **different** plan. Customers cannot switch monthly ↔ annual on their current plan from the portal plan selection screen.

</Aside>

## Change a plan subscription for an organization (B2B) or user (B2C)

1. Open the organization or user record in Kinde.
2. Go to **Organizations** and locate the org, or go to **Users** and locate the user.
3. Select **Billing** in the side menu. Details of the org’s or user’s plan are shown.
4. Select **Change plan**.
5. In the **Choose plan** dialog, plans are listed under their plan group. Select the new plan.
   - The customer’s current plan is labeled **Current plan** and cannot be selected.
   - A plan whose prices are not ready is labeled **Prices are still syncing** and cannot be selected.
6. Select **Save**. The customer’s plan is now updated.

Depending on the [billing policies](/billing/manage-plans/upgrade-downgrade-plans/) you have set, this information will be synced to Stripe and an invoice credit or bill will be issued.

## Change to a plan in a different plan group

When you change a plan in the Kinde admin, you can select any published plan of the same customer type whose prices have finished syncing — including a plan in a different plan group. You can also assign a published plan of the same customer type in a different plan group by creating a new billing agreement via the Management API.

For example, you can move an organization from a plan in your default organization group to a plan in another organization group.

When you do this:

- The customer’s current agreement is cancelled, even if it belongs to a different plan group.
- A new agreement is created for the selected plan.

You cannot assign an organization plan to a user, or a user plan to an organization.

Customers who change their own plan in the self-serve portal still choose from the pricing table, which includes plans from one plan group only.

On the billing details page, the subscription card shows **Plan** and **Next invoice** in the same way as the customer portal (amount and date, including usage recorded so far when the plan has metered features). Agreement ID, Customer ID, and feature counts remain admin-only. See [Billing and invoice cycles](/billing/about-billing/kinde-billing-model/#billing-and-invoice-cycles).
