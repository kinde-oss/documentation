A SAML connection can involve two different certificates, and they are refreshed in different places:

- **Your identity provider's signing certificate.** The IdP uses it to sign the SAML responses it sends to Kinde. Kinde reads it from the IdP metadata. When your IdP rotates this certificate, follow [Keep IdP signing certificates current](/authenticate/enterprise-connections/custom-saml/#keep-idp-signing-certificates-current). For Google Workspace, see [Rotate the Google signing certificate](/authenticate/enterprise-connections/custom-saml-google-workspace/#rotate-the-google-signing-certificate).
- **Kinde's request-signing certificate and private key.** This optional pair, entered in the **Sign SAML request** section of the connection, lets Kinde sign the requests it sends to your IdP. If you set it up, you need to refresh it before it expires. The rest of this page covers that.

## Refresh the request-signing certificate and private key

Your IdP might issue new certificates, or you can generate a certificate and private key yourself. See [Signed certificate and private key](/authenticate/enterprise-connections/custom-saml/#optional-signed-certificate-and-private-key).

1. In Kinde, open the connection:
   - For an environment-level connection, go to **Settings > Environment > Authentication**, scroll to **Enterprise connections**, and select **Configure** on the connection.
   - For an organization-level connection, go to **Organizations**, open the organization, select **Authentication**, and select **Configure** on the connection.
2. Scroll to the **Sign SAML request** section and replace the **Signing certificate** and **Private key** with the new content.
3. Select **Save**.
4. Add the new certificate to your identity provider. The certificate there and the one in Kinde must match exactly.
