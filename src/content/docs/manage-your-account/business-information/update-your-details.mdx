You can change some business details in Kinde, but you may need to contact support for help with other changes.

## Change your sign-in name

1. Sign in to your Kinde dashboard.
2. Open the avatar menu at the bottom left, then select **Profile**.
3. You will be taken to the **Account > Account settings > Profile** page.
4. Change your sign-in name and select **Save**.

    ![kinde update profile sign in name page](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/ab2343bd-5394-46e2-6ea0-e775868da500/socialsharingimage)

## Change the email you sign in to Kinde with

You cannot directly edit the email address you use to sign in to Kinde. However, you can switch your sign-in email by inviting your new email address to the business, promoting it to an Owner, and then removing the old account.

### Add the new email address and promote it to Owner

1. Send a team invitation to the new email address.
2. Accept the invitation via the new email to join the Kinde business.
3. Sign in with your **current** (old) Owner account.
4. On the Kinde home page, select the avatar menu at the bottom left. 
5. In the menu that opens, select **Team members**.
6. Find the new email address in the list and select the **three dots menu** next to it.
7. Select **Edit roles**, choose **Owner**, and save the changes.

### Remove the old email address (if applicable)

1. Sign out, then sign back in using the **new** email address (which now has Owner permissions).
2. On the Kinde home page, select the avatar menu at the bottom left.
3. In the menu that opens, select **Team members**.
4. Find the old email address in the list.
5. Select the **three dots menu** and select **Edit roles**.
6. Change the role from **Owner** to another role (for example, **Admin** or **Member**) and select **Save**.

    <Aside type="warning">

    You must change the role first because Owners cannot remove other Owners.

    </Aside>

7. Once the role is updated, select the **three dots menu** again and select **Remove member**.

## Change business details

1. Sign in to your Kinde dashboard.
2. Open the avatar menu at the bottom left, then select **Business details**.
3. You will be taken to the **Settings > Business settings > Details** page.
4. In the **Basic information** section, you can change the following details:

    - Business name
    - Phone number
    - Email (business email)
    - Kinde perk code
    - Industry
    - Time zone

    ![kinde update business details page](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/a0d599a4-1007-42f6-232a-830c5ed5b100/socialsharingimage)

5. Select **Save**.

## About your business time zone

The time zone you select is used in areas that show dates, such as user history and audit logs.

## Check the data region of your business

The data region is set when you create your business or during Kinde onboarding. To check the data region:

1. Sign in to your Kinde dashboard.
2. Open the avatar menu at the bottom left, then select **Switch business**.

You will be taken to the **All businesses** page where you can see the data region of your business in the business tile.

    ![kinde switch business page](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/d01a2a5a-2cfe-4c7c-5302-2cc447cf7600/socialsharingimage)

By selecting the business URL, you can switch to that business.

## Change invoicing details

If you need to change business details such as tax information, email, or contacts for invoices, email support@kinde.com. Supply your business name, Kinde domain, and the details that need to be changed.
