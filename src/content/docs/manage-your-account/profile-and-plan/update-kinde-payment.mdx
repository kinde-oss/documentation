Kinde’s approach to charging subscription fees is to take the initial base subscription charge immediately for the month ahead, then deduct recurring payments on the monthly anniversary of your sign-up, calculated in arrears for the prior usage period. So if you signed up on 1 December, you are charged immediately for December. Your next bill is issued on 1 January, for usage in December.

Use the procedures below to keep your credit card details up to date.

## Add new payment method

1. Sign in to your Kinde dashboard.
2. Open the avatar menu at the bottom left, then select **Profile**.
3. You will be taken to the **Account > Account settings > Profile** page.
4. Select **Business settings > Payment details**. If you have already entered payment details, they will appear here.

    ![kinde add payment details page](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/417c0df6-26bb-490b-1ceb-d9a1de517400/socialsharingimage)

5. Select **Add payment method**.
6. In the window that appears, add your credit card details and follow the prompts to save.

    <img src="https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/ad6fc679-6485-43c1-9d43-04fca5b09000/socialsharingimage" alt="kinde add payment credit card details page" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" width="300px" />

## Change default payment method

1. Add the new payment details as per the above procedure.
2. Select the **three dots** menu next to the card showing the new details, and select **Set as default**.

## Remove a payment method

If you are on a paid plan, add a new payment method before removing the old method.

In the **Payment details** page, all your payment methods are shown.

1. Select the **three dots** menu next to the payment method you want to remove, then select **Remove**.
2. In the message that appears, confirm that you want to remove the payment method.
