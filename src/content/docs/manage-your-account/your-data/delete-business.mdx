Use this page if you want to **delete a Kinde business**: remove it from Kinde, delete its data, and remove access for everyone.

<Aside type="danger">

Deleting a business cannot be reversed. Only a business **owner** can delete the business.

</Aside>

<Aside>

You can only delete a business yourself if it is on a **free plan**. Businesses on paid plans must contact support to [downgrade first](/manage-your-account/your-data/closing-your-account/#how-to-downgrade-a-paid-plan).

</Aside>

## Before you delete

- [Export your data](/manage-your-account/your-data/exporting-data/)
- Remove any [custom domain](/build/domains/pointing-your-domain/) you have set up
- Tell team members they will lose access to the business

## How to delete a business

To delete a business on a **free plan**:

1. Sign in to the business you want to delete.
2. Go to **Settings > Business > Details**.
3. Scroll to the **Admin actions** section and select **Delete business**.
4. Confirm the action in the dialog by selecting **Delete business**.

    ![kinde delete a business](https://imagedelivery.net/skPPZTHzSlcslvHjesZQcQ/44e881f3-db00-4336-da2c-0dafadb86a00/socialsharingimage)

The business is deleted and can no longer be accessed.

## What happens when you delete a business

- The domain associated with the business cannot be used again unless Kinde later frees it
- All environments, applications, organizations, users, and stored data are deleted
- Owner, admin, and team member access to the business is removed
- Connections and integrations stop working
- Other Kinde businesses you own are not affected
- Team members who have access to multiple businesses can still access those businesses

<Aside type="warning">

If you want to keep the Kinde domain issued to the business, do not delete it. To use your own domain instead, see [custom domain](/build/domains/pointing-your-domain/).

</Aside>
